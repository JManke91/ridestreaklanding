Last updated: 6 September 2026

1. Scope

These terms cover use of the Ride Streak iPhone app, offered by Julian Manke. Purchases of Ride Streak Pro are made and processed through the Apple App Store; Apple's terms apply in addition and take precedence for the purchase itself.

2. What the app does — and does not

Ride Streak analyses rides that have already been recorded. It reads them from Apple Health and, if you enable it, from Strava.

Ride Streak does not record rides. There is no recording feature, no navigation and no route planning. To record you need an Apple Watch, a bike computer or another app that writes to Apple Health.

3. Requirements

Ride Streak requires an iPhone running iOS 18.5 or later. There is no Apple Watch, iPad, Android or web version. Individual features additionally require Apple Health permission, a Strava connection or RideStreak Cloud.

4. Free use and Ride Streak Pro

The app is free to use. Ride Streak Pro is optional and unlocks additional features (training load, power analysis, Fitness & Form, 3D Flyover, image and video export, calories, iCloud sync).
