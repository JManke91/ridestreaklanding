"Strava alternative" is usually the wrong question, because Strava is several very different things at once: a recording app, a social network, a segment competition, a training analysis tool and a data hub. Almost nobody needs all five, and no single app replaces all five well.

A more useful question is: **which of those jobs am I trying to solve?**

## What Strava genuinely does well

Being fair means starting here:

- **The network.** Your friends are on it. That is not a feature another app can rebuild.
- **Segments.** The database has grown over years and is effectively unrivalled.
- **Compatibility.** Practically every device and platform uploads to Strava. As a hub between ecosystems it is the de facto standard.
- **Heatmaps and routing** are genuinely good in the paid tier.

If you are there for the friends or the segments, you have no reason to switch. The rest of this piece is about the other jobs.

## Where Strava does not fit

- **The feed is in the way** when all you want is your own numbers.
- **Per-ride analysis** is thin on the free tier, and even paid it trails specialised tools.
- **It knows nothing about your gear.** There is a gear list, but no real per-component wear tracking with thresholds and status.
- **Your data lives on someone else's servers**, with everything that entails.
- **Comparing you against yourself** is weak: yearly totals yes, but no clean comparison against the equivalent elapsed portion of last year.

## The jobs and the candidates

### Recording

If recording is all you need, the best app is usually the one already on your wrist: **Apple Workout** on the Apple Watch writes straight to Apple Health, needs no account and costs nothing. If you ride with a bike computer you are recording there anyway — more accurately and with better battery life.

Pure recording apps for iPhone are a solved problem. Not worth the switching effort.

### Analysis

This is the gap most people mean when they search for a Strava alternative. Candidates range from full training platforms with prices to match, down to lean statistics apps.

For iPhone riders already recording into Apple Health, the decisive question is **whether the app can read from Health** — otherwise you record twice or maintain data by hand.

### Training guidance

If you steer by CTL, ATL and TSB you traditionally end up on one of the big training platforms. They do a lot, cost considerably more, and mostly assume a power meter for the numbers to mean much. For riders without one, the question is whether a heart-rate-based estimate is clearly labelled as an estimate.

### Gear and maintenance

This is the thinnest field. There are maintenance apps you type kilometres into by hand, which in practice go quiet after three weeks. The difference is whether the kilometres arrive **automatically** from your rides.

## Where Ride Streak sits

To be clear: **Ride Streak is not a recording app and not a social network.** It records nothing, it does not navigate, it does not plan routes, and there is no feed. If that is what you want, it is the wrong tool.

It covers the two jobs where Strava is weakest:

- **Analysis.** Stats for day, week, month and year, every number compared against the equivalent elapsed portion of last year. Goals, weekly streak, personal records. Per ride: a speed-coloured map, a scrubable elevation profile, real heart-rate zones, 5 km splits, laps and segments. With Pro, training load, power analysis and Fitness &amp; Form on top.
- **The Garage.** Bikes and wearing parts with your own thresholds, kilometres counted automatically from the assigned rides, status from healthy to overdue.

Plus: no accounts, no ads, data on the device. And it works **with** Strava rather than against it — an optional connection pulls in the power, cadence, splits, segments and laps that never reach Apple Health.

## In short

| You want … | Sensible choice |
|---|---|
| friends, kudos, chasing segments | Strava, no argument |
| to record | Apple Watch, Garmin, Wahoo |
| honest distance comparisons, goals, records | an analysis app built on Apple Health |
| to train by CTL/ATL/TSB | a training platform, or Ride Streak Pro without needing a power meter |
| to know when the chain is due | an app that counts kilometres per component automatically |
| your data off other people's servers | an app working locally on Apple Health |

For most people this does not end in a switch but in a combination: keep recording as you do, keep Strava for the social parts — and do the analysis where it is better.
